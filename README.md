# Computer Modeling in Materials Science

This repository contains my implementations, notebooks, and scripts for the **Computer Modeling** course in the Master's Program at **Sabancı University**.

---

## Course & Personal Information

* **University:** Sabancı University
* **Program:** Master of Science
* **Course:** MAT 504 — Computer Modelling in Materials Science
* **Instructor:** Prof. Betul Canimkurbey (Kilavuz)
* **Author:** Hossein Moein

---

## Main Objectives & Scope

1. **Molecular Dynamics (MD) & Sampling Algorithms:** Building fundamental MD and Monte Carlo (MC) equations, transition rules, and integration loops from scratch.
2. **Analysis & Diagnostics:** Developing customized visualization tools to generate publication-ready plots that match standard output formats from molecular simulation packages such as **GROMACS**.
3. **Equilibration & Convergence:** Evaluating burn-in periods, autocorrelation times, mixing, and detailed balance across complex energy landscapes (e.g., double-well potentials).
4. **Machine Learning Applications:** Integrating ML and computational data-driven approaches to solve problems in **Biophysics**, **Biological Systems**, and **Materials Science**.
